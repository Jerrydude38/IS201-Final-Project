Disney!

In 2012, Disney purchased Lucasfilm along with the intellectual property of Star Wars. Disney then created the sequel trilogy and other Star Wars movies and shows such as Obi-Wan Kenobi, The Mandelorian, The Bad Batch, and more.

Despite this, common sentiment is that Disney has "ruined" Star Wars. This sentiment is primarily felt around the sequel triology with audiences feeling that the story telling and development were disappointing.

This is an interview with Star Wars creator George Lucas. In this video he shares his thoughts on the sequel trilogy, why he gave up ownership of the series, and more. We highly recommend watching this video to better understand this important historical aspect of Star Wars.
